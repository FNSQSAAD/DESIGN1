What happens on the call

Fifteen minutes, four questions

  1. What are you trying to do — buy, refinance, invest, build, or fund something for a business.
  2. What does the income look like — payslips, self-employment, or a mix.
  3. What is already owed — car, cards, personal loans, an existing mortgage.
  4. When does this need to happen — a contract next week is a different conversation from “sometime next year”.

At the end you will have a realistic range, the likely obstacles, and a clear next step — which is sometimes “wait, and here is what to fix first”.
