How we work

Four things you can hold us to

01

We must by law put your interests first

The best interests duty in the National Consumer Credit Protection Act applies to every credit assistance recommendation we make to you. It is not a marketing line; it is the legal standard we are held to, and it is enforceable.

02

You see how we are paid before you apply

Lenders pay a commission when a loan settles, and the amount differs between lenders. You receive a written Credit Guide showing the range before you apply for anything, so you can weigh our recommendation with that in front of you.

03

We will tell you not to do it

If refinancing costs more than it saves, if the purchase is a stretch, or if waiting six months puts you in a materially better position, that is the advice you get — in writing, with the numbers behind it.

04

There is somewhere to complain that is not us

Tell us first. If we cannot resolve it, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority reviews the complaint independently at no cost to you, and its determinations bind us. That is a real check, not a courtesy.

What we are not

The limits, stated plainly

We arrange credit. We are not financial advisers, accountants, tax agents, conveyancers or buyers’ agents, and we will not pretend to be when the question strays into their territory.

  • Tax and negative gearing are questions for your accountant. We can tell you how a lender treats an income figure; we cannot tell you what to claim.
  • Whether to buy a particular property is not a credit question. We can tell you what it would cost to fund it.
  • Nothing on this website is credit assistance. It is general information. Advice for your situation happens in a conversation, on the record, with your figures in front of us.
  • Access to a lender panel is not a recommendation. Being accredited with a lender does not mean that lender suits you, and we will explain which ones we set aside and why.
