Why use a broker at all

A bank can only offer you a bank.

Going direct means one credit policy, one set of products and one answer. A broker compares a panel — and is legally required to act in your interests, not a lender’s.

  • One conversation, many credit policiesThe same application read by lenders who assess income, bonus, overtime and self-employment very differently.
  • We tell you when not to moveIf switching costs more than it saves once discharge, valuation and lender fees are counted, we will say so and put it in writing.
  • How we get paid, up frontLenders pay commission on settlement. You receive a written Credit Guide showing what we may be paid before you apply for anything.
  • Somewhere to complain that is not usIf we get it wrong, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority reviews it independently and at no cost to you.

Ask your question
What does this cost me?

For most residential home loans, nothing. The lender pays us a commission when the loan settles. Our service to you is free, but other fees and lender charges may apply — application fees, valuation fees, settlement fees and, on some files, lenders mortgage insurance.

You receive a written Credit Guide setting out what we may be paid before you apply for anything. Some commercial and specialist files do carry a fee, and if yours does, you will be told in writing before any work starts.

Will an enquiry hurt my credit score?

No. A conversation, a calculator and a shortlist involve no credit enquiry at all. A formal credit enquiry only happens when you decide to lodge an application with a specific lender, and we will tell you before that point is reached.

Why will you not show me a rate on this website?

Because the rate you would actually be offered depends on the lender, the product, your deposit, your credit history and how the lender reads your income. A headline number on a website tells you almost nothing about that, and quoting one without the comparison rate and warnings required by the National Consumer Credit Protection Act would be misleading.

Our calculators let you enter the rate you have been quoted, so the arithmetic is yours.

How long does it take?

It depends on the lender, the complexity of your income and how quickly documents come back. We give you an expected timeline at the shortlist stage and update it if it changes. verify typical timeframes before publishing

I am self-employed. Is that a problem?

It is a different conversation, not a harder one. Lenders differ enormously in how they treat company profit, add-backs, trust distributions and a short trading history. Some will consider one year of returns; a few will look at business bank statements instead. Which lenders those are changes, so it is worth asking rather than assuming.

What if I am unhappy with something?

Tell us first and we will try to fix it. If we cannot, you can take it to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority, which reviews complaints independently and at no cost to you. We are a member and its decisions bind us.
